The 20 Web Design Principles Applied

1. Readable & Web-Friendly Fonts Inter typography with Noto Sans Gujarati & Devanagari fallbacks for clean legibility across devices.
2. Utilize the 'F' Pattern Structure headers, formulas, and step-by-step text to match natural eye scanning on content-heavy pages.
3. Or the 'Z' Pattern Diagonal flow on landing and hero views leading directly to key calls-to-action.
4. Use Negative Space Generous padding around canvas and cards to prevent cognitive clutter.
5. Keep Design Consistent Unified design tokens, button states, icon set, and color palette across all pages.
6. Simple & Logical Page Navigation Clear top menu and breadcrumbs guiding users seamlessly without overloading them.
7. Complementary Color Palette Restrained deep navy, indigo, and accent mapping (Blue = calculus, Violet = geometry).
8. Keep the Audience in Mind Tailored specifically for GSEB Std 11 & Std 12 Science students and teachers.
9. Optimize Buttons & CTAs High-contrast, prominent buttons with clear next-step labels.
10. Maintain Visual Hierarchy Mathematical canvas always takes visual priority over auxiliary panels.
11. Pay Attention to Details Refined focus rings, smooth transitions, and precise mathematical typography.
12. Fitts's Law Primary touch targets are minimum 44×44px for easy tapping on mobile screens.
13. Choose Images Wisely Only purposeful mathematical visual diagrams and live interactive viewports.
14. Prioritize User Experience Read → Understand → See → Change → Calculate → Practise learning flow.
15. Consider Using Grid Systems Structured 12-column desktop and 4-column mobile layout grids.
16. Avoid Big Chunks of Text Scannable cards, bulleted lists, and clear KaTeX equations.
17. Use Invariance Highlighting primary active tools visually while keeping standard controls uniform.
18. Hick's Law Grouped basic and advanced tools to prevent decision overload.
19. Use Symmetry Balanced panel distribution across central axes.
20. Design for Web & Mobile Fully responsive layouts adjusting smoothly across smartphone, tablet, and desktop monitors.
